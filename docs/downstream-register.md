## Entry maintenance

When work becomes concrete, add a brief link, source files and commit, adaptation changes, intended audience, receiving owner, review status and publication/use authority. Record the actual destination only when known and appropriate for this public register. A private destination may be described without publishing its path or contents.

Retain stable IDs. Record source changes requiring reassessment and when the receiving owner confirms the response. Use states such as intended, scoped, in development, under review, authorized for stated use, or superseded only when the corresponding evidence exists. Source package publication alone does not advance an adaptation's state.

The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) plans the downstream work; the [backlog](../BACKLOG.md) tracks actions. This register owns the relationship and source-version record, not the receiving team's work queue.
