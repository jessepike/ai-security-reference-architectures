## A small exploration record

Each note needs a question, a status (exploring or hypothesis), the canonical files and version it starts from, the proposed change, evidence and counterevidence, and what result would support or reject the idea. Link it to a backlog item when it becomes active work.

Exploration may question the existing model. It does not change the model merely by being newer or more detailed. To promote a result, record the proposed decision and rationale, review the affected meaning, obtain the relevant owner direction, update the canonical source and its dependent outputs, and identify downstream adaptations needing review. Preserve the note and disposition as evidence.

Only source-authorized, public-safe content belongs here. Confidential examples and internal commercial material remain in their authorized contexts. No bulk import from adjacent workspaces is implied.
