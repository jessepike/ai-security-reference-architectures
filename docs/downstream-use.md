# Using the canonical package downstream

The [governing intent](../intent.md) identifies this as our canonical AI Security & Governance package and a basis for future ePlus AI Ignite and AI Security & Governance GTM work. This document explains how that relationship is maintained. The [downstream register](downstream-register.md) records intended and actual adaptations.

## What comes from this package

Use the [source map](../content/source-map.md) to locate the current definitions, shared story, three security views, governance relationship, examples, architect rationale and authoring conventions. These provide the foundation for an adaptation. The website, PNGs, PDFs and presentation are publication formats of this package, rather than separate sources of architectural authority.

An adaptation may change the depth, sequence, examples and vocabulary needed by an audience. It may add channel context or a specific business conversation. Record those changes so another author can distinguish the shared foundation from the added context. Changing a core definition or architecture meaning requires a proposal to this package and an explicit source decision.

## Intended ePlus use

| Intended use | Contribution from this package | Additional work that must be established in its own context |
|---|---|---|
| ePlus AI Ignite | Shared orientation, awareness, vocabulary, example questions and explanation of security/governance responsibilities. | Exact audience, stage of the conversation, receiving owner, approved program description and artifact scope. |
| ePlus AI Security & Governance GTM | Consistent narrative and architecture rationale that can support audience-specific conversations and material. | Approved positioning, offers, product/service claims, commercial terms, delivery capability, evidence and external-use authority. |

These are the owner's intended uses of the package. No specific ePlus adaptation, adoption, approved offer or delivery commitment is established by this record. The receiving work remains responsible for its claims and approvals. Creating this interface does not import another workspace or authorize publishing confidential material.

## Before an adaptation begins

Record a short brief and a register entry containing:

1. The audience, intended outcome, receiving owner and destination artifact or workspace.
2. The source files and exact source commit or release used, plus the applicable intent version.
3. The definitions and meanings retained, proposed changes, and any new claims requiring evidence.
4. The source review limitations that matter to the adaptation, and the review appropriate to its intended use.
5. The person who decides whether it may be used or published in the receiving context.

For now, unselected fields remain “not selected” or “not established.” A future author must resolve them before making corresponding claims; their absence does not prevent maintaining the canonical package.

## Returning learning and managing change

An adaptation can reveal an unclear explanation, a missing scenario or a design weakness. Return the generalizable finding with its evidence as a [backlog](../BACKLOG.md) item. Keep customer-specific or confidential details in the authorized receiving context. Explore candidate changes in the [exploration area](exploration/README.md), with their source version and hypothesis visible.

When a source change is accepted, record the decision, revise the canonical Markdown and update the affected renders. Then use the register to identify adaptations that need an impact review. Their owners decide how to update and revalidate their material. Do not silently rewrite a downstream artifact or assume its previous approval carries over.

This creates a continuing relationship: **the canonical package informs adaptations; evidence from adaptations can improve the canonical package.** The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) and backlog make the work visible. Publication and internal source authority do not substitute for a reuse-license decision or approval in another organization.
