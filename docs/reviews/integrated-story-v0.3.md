# Integrated story v0.3 review

9 September 2026 · Bounded model-assisted narrative review

## Scope

A separate reviewing agent compared overview v0.3 with the unchanged AI Governance v0.1 canonical source and detailed guide. The review checked narrative coherence, plain language, coordination and decision rights, security input before approval, continuing governance, both directions of the evidence exchange, incident authority, the supplier-payment example and version-specific review claims.

The reviewer found no blocking contradiction. One advisory finding concerned whether the statement that the exchange applies to all three security views could imply that an attacker's AI must enter the enterprise AI-use governance lifecycle.

## Finding and disposition

STORY-R01, advisory: distinguish governance of the organization's own AI uses from ordinary security and business authority for responding to AI-enabled attacks when the organization has no AI use.

The author revised the paragraph beginning “The relationship takes a different form in each security view.” It now makes the distinction explicit and explains that attacks affecting the organization's AI still return evidence for decisions about that use. The same reviewer read the revised paragraph and confirmed the finding resolved, with no remaining finding on that point.

## Limits

This review covers the narrative and its consistency with the existing governance sources. It is separate from the original Fable review and from the earlier v0.2 overview/governance-package review. It did not review the updated presentation or website renders, repeat the PNG/PDF reviews, assess deployed controls or establish human acceptance. Reader testing and human architectural review remain open. The final source hash is recorded in the release manifest; render and publication checks are recorded separately.
