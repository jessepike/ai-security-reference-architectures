# Roadmap

This is a directional plan for [AISG-INTENT-001](intent.md). It orders learning and evidence needs without setting dates, assigning enterprise owners or commissioning future work. Backlog IDs are the maintained work record; each item below links to its evidence and owner state there.

## RM-01 — Canonical foundation

**State:** Established

Maintain one vendor-neutral AI Security & Governance package that gives readers a coherent starting point and an inspectable reference. The existing foundation includes the three peer security views, the governance companion, canonical sources, five PNGs, four detailed guides, a 44-slide presentation and the public website.

**Outcome trace:** O1 — Orientation and awareness; O2 — Education and practical reference; with the record structure supporting O3 and O4.

**Evidence of progress:** [AISG-001](BACKLOG.md#aisg-001--canonical-package-foundation), [AISG-002](BACKLOG.md#aisg-002--integrated-governance-story) and [AISG-003](BACKLOG.md#aisg-003--project-record-alignment). The current story content release is `54a2036ff85a89a0684cd834e7d77a849a3b5376`; its artifact/hash record is the [release manifest](docs/release-manifest.json). [AISG-010](BACKLOG.md#aisg-010--project-guide-website-exposure-check) records completed project-guide exposure checks for the project-record release.

The package’s local repository separation and relocation are tracked in [AISG-013](BACKLOG.md#aisg-013--workspace-relocation-and-repository-separation). They preserve the canonical foundation and its public deployment boundary.

## RM-02 — Confidence and reader education

**State:** Next proposed work

Build evidence that readers can understand and question the package: disposition the original review findings, obtain human architectural review, test comprehension with intended readers, improve public-facing clarity through the supported cleanup process, and resolve reuse terms.

**Outcome trace:** O1 — Orientation and awareness; O2 — Education and practical reference.

**Work and evidence:** [AISG-004](BACKLOG.md#aisg-004--adjudicate-original-architecture-review-findings), [AISG-005](BACKLOG.md#aisg-005--obtain-human-architectural-review), [AISG-006](BACKLOG.md#aisg-006--test-intended-reader-comprehension), [AISG-008](BACKLOG.md#aisg-008--complete-external-writing-cleanup) and [AISG-009](BACKLOG.md#aisg-009--decide-reuse-licensing). A published or model-reviewed artifact is not sufficient evidence for these outcomes.

## RM-03 — Exploration and evolution

**State:** Future

Use the canonical package as a controlled starting point for new questions, evidence and environment-specific exploration. A proposed exploration can challenge the package but can alter it only through a reviewed source edit and recorded decision.

**Outcome trace:** O3 — Evolution and exploration.

**Work and evidence:** [AISG-007](BACKLOG.md#aisg-007--tailor-to-a-selected-environment) is the currently open prerequisite for any environment-specific work. [AISG-011](BACKLOG.md#aisg-011--exploration-intake-and-canon-improvement-loop) is not commissioned; it calls for a first scoped exploration using the [documented intake and decision path](docs/exploration/README.md).

## RM-04 — Downstream adaptations

**State:** Intended, not yet commissioned

Future ePlus AI Ignite and AI Security & Governance GTM materials may draw on this package when their own authorized work names the source revision, audience/context changes, receiving owner, review state and route for feedback. They remain adaptations, not evidence that this package is an approved ePlus offer or that any enterprise decision has been made.

**Outcome trace:** O4 — Downstream foundation.

**Work and evidence:** [AISG-012](BACKLOG.md#aisg-012--downstream-adaptation-intake) is not commissioned. The [downstream-use contract](docs/downstream-use.md) and [register](docs/downstream-register.md) are the maintained records for a future scoped adaptation.
