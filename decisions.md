## Review boundary

The [intent](intent.md) governs direction. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [backlog](BACKLOG.md) describe the work proposed or undertaken against it; [status](status.md) reports current state. Entries in those work records do not automatically become accepted decisions. The [project guide](content/project-guide.md) connects all maintained records.

The original three component packages received model-assisted critique with material reservations. New content and successful browser/build checks do not inherit that review. Keep current coverage and open questions in [review status](content/review-status.md).
